Before you write

  • User guide and FAQ.
  • An order is missing: it counts only once it's fully paid, in the month of the last payment, and only if it was placed for a B2B company that has a rep.
  • The amount looks different from the order total: commission is on the subtotal after discounts and returns, without shipping and tax.
  • "Paid B2B orders have no sales rep": assign those companies in the Companies tab.

Billing

Plans are billed by Shopify. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from the app; you don't need to contact us.
