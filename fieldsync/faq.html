Frequently asked questions

Field Sync and Rollups for Zoho CRM

Which Zoho CRM editions are supported?

Standard, Professional, Enterprise, Ultimate, CRM Plus and Zoho One.

Where do I create rules?

In the Sync Rules tab (it may be under the "…" menu in the top bar). Each record is one rule.

When do rules run?

Instantly when an Account, Contact or Deal is created or edited, right after you save a rule (to cover existing records), and every day.

Does it work on custom modules?

Yes. Fill in the Related List field with the API name of the related list. Custom modules are updated when a rule is saved and in the daily recalculation.

Does my data leave Zoho?

No. Everything runs inside your own Zoho CRM. There are no external servers.

Can it cause loops?

No. Updates made by the extension do not trigger other workflows.

What are the limits?

Up to 1,000 child records per parent in real time, and up to 2,000 parents per module per recalculation.

A value looks wrong. What should I do?

Open the rule and check its Last Run and Last Result fields, confirm the field API names, and email nudge4.support@gmail.com.

How do I stop a rule?

Untick Active on the rule.
