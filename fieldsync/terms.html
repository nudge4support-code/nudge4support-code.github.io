Field Sync and Rollups for Zoho CRM · Last updated: 5 October 2026

Field Sync and Rollups is a Zoho CRM extension that copies field values between related records and calculates rollup values according to rules you create.

You are responsible for the rules you create and for checking that the results are correct for your business. Test new rules on a small set of records first. The extension updates field values in your CRM, so keep regular backups of your CRM data.
