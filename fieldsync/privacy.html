Privacy policy

Field Sync and Rollups for Zoho CRM · Last updated: 5 October 2026

What the extension does with data

It reads and updates records in your own Zoho CRM account, only as described by the Sync Rules you create.

Where data goes

Nowhere else. All processing runs inside Zoho CRM using Zoho's own functions. The extension has no external servers and makes no calls to third-party services. Nudge4 cannot see your CRM data.

What is stored

Your rules are stored as records in the Sync Rules module inside your CRM. Nothing is stored outside your Zoho account.

Personal data

The extension does not collect, sell or share personal data. Any personal data in your CRM stays under your control and Zoho's privacy terms.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling removes the extension's components. Field values that rules already wrote remain in your records.
